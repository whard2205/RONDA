Plant operators walk inspection rounds and write gauge readings on paper — hours before anyone checks them. RONDA lets them just talk, then tells them on the spot when a reading has crossed its limit. The server does the checking, never the model.

How to try it

Press start, allow the microphone, then speak — no typing, no menus.

  1. 1Name a machine — Pump three A
  2. 2Say the number — Seventy eight
  3. 3It reads back the value, the limit and last shift
